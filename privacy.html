Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how the "Albin Test Page" Messenger assistant (the "Service") handles information when you send a message to our Facebook Page.

What we collect

When you message our Page, we receive the text of your message and your Messenger sender ID (a number Facebook assigns to your account for this Page). If you choose to share your name, phone number or contact preferences in the chat, we receive that too. We do not receive your Facebook password or your private profile information.

How we use it

We use your messages only to reply to your enquiries about items listed on our Page, and to let our team follow up with you when you ask for it. The assistant is automated. Your message text, together with recent messages from the same chat and the text of our Page posts, is sent to a third-party AI provider (OpenAI) to generate a reply.

Storage and retention

Recent chat messages are kept temporarily in the Service's memory to keep the conversation coherent and are cleared when the Service restarts. Server logs may briefly record messages for troubleshooting or when a conversation is handed to a human. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share message content only with Meta (Facebook/Messenger, to send and receive messages) and OpenAI (to generate replies), and only as needed to operate the Service. We do not share it with anyone else unless required by law.

Your choices

You can stop messaging us at any time. To ask us to delete any information we hold about you, contact us at the email below and include your Facebook name so we can find the conversation.
